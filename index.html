Setup

Core Box steps with the Grimlash and Shady Acres additions folded in, in table order.

  1. Open both halves of the box

    Pop off the Grimlash cover (its reverse is the Killer board) and the Shady Acres cover (Location board). Pull out both component trays.

  2. Pick your Final Girl

    Choose or draw a Final Girl card. This box adds Vicky and Joy; any Final Girl you own works. Move new Final Girl cards into the Core Box for storage.

  3. Player board

    Place the Player board, normal side up (Extreme Horror Mode is the harsher back side: tougher Horror track and only 5 Time). Put your Final Girl card beneath it, Victim Saved side showing. Set the Time marker on 6.

  4. Dice and Action cards

    Put the six dice aside. Pull the six Zero Cost Action cards (gold hourglass showing 0): that is your starting hand. Sort the rest by name into piles in two rows. This is the Action Tableau.

  5. Supplies

    Set aside Victim meeples, Special Victim meeples, the three Victim Overflow boards with their tokens, Health markers, Tracking markers and the Bloodlust marker.

  6. Lay out the boards

    Grimlash’s board goes above the Player board; Shady Acres goes directly to its right. Bloodlust marker on the bottom of Grimlash’s Bloodlust track.

  7. Finale and Dark Power Grimlash

    Shuffle Grimlash’s Finale cards (upper-body backs) and place one facedown on the board. Shuffle his Dark Power cards (lower-body backs) and place one facedown below it. Return the rest to the tray.

    The Epic Finale and Epic Dark Power (bloody borders) are only for when you want a brutal game. Leave them out otherwise.

  8. Terror deck

    Shuffle Grimlash’s and Shady Acres’ Terror cards together and deal 10 facedown. That is the Terror deck; set the leftovers aside unseen.

  9. Item decks Shady Acres

    Shuffle the Shady Acres Item cards and deal three facedown piles of four above the board, one per Search space (Ralph’s Garage, the Old Barn, the McCoy Farmhouse). Flip the top card of each pile faceup. Return the extra Items to the tray.

  10. Setup card Shady Acres

    Draw one of the five Setup cards and place the Final Girl, Grimlash and yellow Victims as shown. Shady Acres setups use 14 Victims rather than the usual 12. Keep the Setup card visible above the board.

  11. Final Health tokens

    Mix the nine black Final Health tokens facedown. Without looking, put one on Grimlash’s board and one on your Player board. Return the others unseen.

  12. Health and Horror

    Grimlash has 10 health: his black token plus 9 Health markers. Your Final Girl gets health equal to her card, counting her black token. Grimlash’s starting Horror is 4: set your Horror marker there.

  13. Harvest Madness Grimlash

    Place the Harvest Madness marker at the start of its track. Sort the 15 Harvest Madness cards into separate shuffled decks for levels 1, 2 and 3.

    The exact start space and where the track sits: check Grimlash’s rules sheet.

  14. Children and Curses Shady Acres

    Put the Children card beside the board. Place one Child on the Altar of the Old Gods (northeast corner); keep the other two in the supply. Shuffle the 8 Ancient Curse cards facedown nearby.

  15. First Event

    Shuffle the 10 Shady Acres Event cards facedown and reveal the top one. Apply it, then start turn one.

The turn

Five phases, in order. Rotten Harvest steps are marked.

1

Action phase

Play Action cards one at a time: move, search, rest, attack and so on. Most require a Horror Roll. Stop when you choose, run out of cards, a card ends the phase, or Time drops below zero.

Any time before the phase ends, discard Action cards for +1 Time each.

2

Planning phase

  1. Spend remaining Time to buy cards from the Tableau (cost in the card’s lower right). No buying cards played or discarded since the last Planning phase, no going below zero Time, hand limit 10.
  2. Reset Time to 6. Unspent Time is lost.
  3. Return all cards played or discarded this turn to their Tableau piles.

Zero Cost cards in the Tableau can always be taken, even at zero Time, as long as you stay at 10 cards or fewer.

3

Killer phase

  1. Shady AcresChildren act first. Each Child resolves fully before the next, starting with the one nearest the Altar. See Children.
  2. GrimlashKiller Action from the Finale card. Before the Finale: target the closest Victim or Final Girl, move 1 space, attack. If that attack killed a Victim, +1 Harvest Madness.
  3. Draw a Terror card and resolve it top to bottom (skip once the Finale is revealed).
4

Panic phase

Only happens if a Victim died this round. Victims in Grimlash’s space roll a die each and may scatter along the numbered paths. Full walkthrough in Panic and end-of-round reset.

5

Upkeep phase

  1. Finale check. If the Terror deck is empty, reveal the Finale (and the Dark Power, if still facedown).
  2. Shady AcresPlace a new Child on the Altar if one is left in the supply.
  3. Resolve any upkeep from Events, Items, Curses or Dark Powers (for example Rite of the Corrupted).
  4. Rearrange items between your hands and backpack if you want.

Harvest Madness

Grimlash’s signature system. It makes you stronger until it kills you.

Rising

Madness goes up when Grimlash’s Killer Action kills a Victim, and through many Grimlash Terror cards, Minor Dark Powers, Dark Powers and some Bloodlust spaces.

Only a Victim killed by the Grimlash attack just resolved raises it through his Killer Action; Children sacrifices don’t (designer ruling).

New level, new power

Each time the marker reaches a new level, draw 2 cards from that level’s Harvest Madness deck and keep 1. Higher levels give stronger powers.

Lowering it

Whenever you gain hearts (healing), you may instead lower Harvest Madness. It is one or the other for that whole effect: you can’t split one heal between health and Madness.

Dropping below a level discards that level’s card. Climbing back up draws a fresh one.

Past level 3

If Harvest Madness would go beyond level 3 onto the skull, you lose immediately. Your Final Health token doesn’t save you.

Grimlash

An old harvest god woken by sacrifice. Slow but relentless.

Stats Grimlash

  • Health 10. Starting Horror 4.
  • Pre-Finale Killer Action: target closest Victim or Final Girl, move 1, attack, +1 Madness if a Victim died.
  • Bloodlust raises his speed and damage and triggers extra Madness at certain spaces. Read the row each time it climbs.

Only Terror cards move him far before the Finale, so his position is fairly predictable. Use that.

Dark Powers Grimlash

All tie into Harvest Madness. Revealed when Bloodlust reaches the marked space.

  • Rite of the Corrupted: if Madness didn’t rise during the turn, it rises at the end of the turn anyway.
  • Rite of the Old One: every time Madness reaches a new level, discard a Terror card, pulling the Finale closer.
The third Dark Power and the Epic Dark Power aren’t summarized here; read the card when revealed.

Finales Grimlash

After the Finale he stops crawling one space at a time.

  • Withered Harbinger: charges at you and attacks twice; you lose Time for spaces he moves through.
  • Ancient Abomination: his attacks on you hit harder.

Minor Dark Powers Grimlash

Drawn from the Terror deck, placed above his board with their own Health markers. Your damage strips those markers first; clearing one discards it.

  • Flaming Eyes: keeps pushing your Madness up. Kill it early.
  • Burning Sickle: kills Victims and hits you in the same strike.

Terror cards Grimlash

Many raise Madness, punish you for being at a certain level, or simply add Horror. Keep Horror under control; you rarely keep an extra die for long.

Shady Acres

Dirt roads, corn rows and an altar to something old.

The map Shady Acres

A road runs from the bottom right to the top left, with corn field and pasture spaces off it. All three Search spaces sit off the main route: reaching Ralph’s Garage from the road takes a detour, and the McCoy Farmhouse connects only through the top-left exit space (then the Cow Pasture and a corn field). Plan routes before you commit Time.

The Altar of the Old Gods is in the northeast.

Children Shady Acres

  • Children are Enemies, not Minions. They and Grimlash never attack each other.
  • One starts on the Altar; one more arrives there each Upkeep, up to the 3 meeples.
  • In the Killer phase, before Grimlash, each Child: targets the closest Victim or Final Girl, moves 1, then tries to abduct.
  • Resolve one Child completely before the next, starting nearest the Altar.
  • Abduct: roll a die. On 1 or 2, the Child and one Victim go to the Altar and the Victim is sacrificed: Bloodlust rises as normal, and the Victim goes on top of the Ancient Curse deck. One attempt per Child, however many Victims share its space.
  • You may kill Children, but they come back. You don’t need to defeat them to win.
Child health, what a Child does when it reaches you, and how killed Children return: check the Children card.

Ancient Curses Shady Acres

When the third sacrificed Victim lands on the Curse deck, draw an Ancient Curse. Curses stay for the rest of the game: faster Victim deaths, faster Children, Time loss in corn fields and similar. The Cornhusk Effigy (and a few other effects) can remove one.

The 8 Curses: Chthonic Possession, Dark Fields, Decaying Earth, Gnarled Stalks, Malevolent Aura, Omen of Blood, Omen of Death, Omen of Plague.

Items worth knowing Shady Acres

Five of the Items are weapons, which is a lot for one location.

  • Pilgrim’s Musket: ranged, but not while next to a corn field.
  • Scythe: in a corn field, split damage across Enemies there and clear the space so it is no longer a corn field.
  • Cowboy Boots: twice, move 2 spaces without playing an Action card.
  • Combine Keys: harvest the big corn fields and add 1 space to Walk (not Sprint).
  • Children’s Toys: helps against the Children.
  • Cornhusk Effigy: remove an Ancient Curse.

Special Victims (Events) Shady Acres

  • Sally McCoy: passive on the board; saving her gives the Combine Keys, or Time if you already have them.
  • Ralph the Mechanic: saving him adds a use to your limited-use Items. No penalty if he dies.
  • Harvest Cultist: walks toward you, then drags you both toward Grimlash while with you. Save him fast for Horror reduction. No penalty if he dies.

Other Events: Acres of Hatred, Festering Rot, It Hungers for Us, It Lurks Between the Rows, “The fields are moving...” and more.

Shady Acres Terror cards Shady Acres

Several move or activate the Children on their own, and some add new Victims. Nobody Knows Where They Take Them sacrifices without a die roll.

Vicky and Joy

Both give Time for saving Victims and an extra 1-space move. Vicky also gives hearts and unlocks her Ultimate at 5 saves (a situational movement boost that costs Time). Joy gives Horror reduction and unlocks at 6 saves: once per turn, ignore one hit or heart loss.

Joy’s Horror relief pairs well with Grimlash; Vicky’s hearts give you more chances to push Madness down.

Panic and end-of-round reset

What happens after the Killer phase, and what carries over into the next round.

Does panic happen this round? Core

Panic needs both of these:

  • At least one Victim was killed this round. Where, how and by whom doesn’t matter: Grimlash’s attack, a Terror card, an Event, or a Children sacrifice all count.
  • The Victim is in the same space as Grimlash.

No deaths this round means no panic at all, even if Victims are standing next to him. Victims elsewhere on the board never panic in this phase. You, the Final Girl, don’t panic in the Panic phase.

Panic numbers on the board Core

Each line connecting two spaces has panic numbers at both ends: one set on each space’s side of the line.

Use the numbers in the space the Victim is standing in (the end of the line nearest its current space). The set at the other end belongs to the neighbouring space and is only used when a Victim panics out of that space.

How to resolve panic Core

  1. Pick one panicking Victim (you choose the order).
  2. Roll 1 die for that Victim.
  3. Look only at the numbers printed in the Victim’s current space, at its end of each line. Find the rolled number and move the Victim one space along that line.
  4. If no path out of the space shows that number, the Victim stays where it is.
  5. Repeat for each Victim in Grimlash’s space, one die each.

Each Victim moves at most one space. Panicked Victims can run into danger, including another Enemy’s space, and one that lands on an Exit is not saved: only you can save Victims, during your Action phase.

Victims on a Victim Overflow board for Grimlash’s space count as being in that space and panic too.

Panic from cards Core

Some Terror cards, Events or other effects make Victims panic at other times, sometimes even you. Resolve it exactly the same way: roll, check the paths out of that space, move one space or stay.

Children and panic Shady Acres

With Minions, Victims sharing a Minion’s space also panic. The Children are Enemies but not Minions, so this doesn’t automatically apply to them.

Whether Victims in a Child’s space panic: check the Children card and the Shady Acres sheet.

What resets each round Core

  • Time goes back to 6 in the Planning phase, after you finish buying. Unspent Time is lost.
  • Played and discarded Action cards go back to their Tableau piles at the end of Planning, after buying. That’s why you can’t rebuy a card you just used.
  • Cards you play outside the Action phase (Reaction cards in the Killer phase, for example) wait in the discard pile until the end of the next Planning phase.
  • The “did a Victim die this round” check for panic, and the “did Madness rise this round” check for Rite of the Corrupted, start fresh.

What carries over Core

  • Your hand. Unplayed cards stay with you. There’s no automatic draw: you only get new cards by buying in Planning, taking Zero Cost cards, or rewards.
  • Health, Horror, Bloodlust, Items and where everyone stands.
  • Minor Dark Powers, until their health is cleared.
  • Ongoing Events, unless the card says when to discard it.
  • GrimlashHarvest Madness level and its cards.
  • Shady AcresChildren on the board, the sacrifice count toward the next Curse, and every Curse drawn.

Upkeep cleanup Core

Upkeep is the last chance to tidy before the next round: Finale check, new Child on the Altar, end-of-round card effects, then rearrange Items between hands and backpack. That rearrange is the only free one; otherwise you can only rearrange when you gain an Item.

Core rules

The Core Box rules that apply to every Feature Film.

Winning and losing Core

Kill Grimlash and you win. Die and you lose. If both happen at once, you still win. At Shady Acres you also lose instantly if Harvest Madness passes level 3. Saving Victims is optional, but it fuels rewards and your Ultimate Ability.

Horror Rolls Core

  • Roll dice equal to your Horror Level (always at least 1).
  • 5–6 is a success. 3–4 is a partial: discard 2 Action cards to turn it into a success, once per die.
  • No successes means failure. Resolve the card’s line for 2+ successes, 1 success or failure, left to right.
  • More than 6 dice: note results and re-roll to cover the extra.

Horror track ends Core

Lowering Horror past the bottom gives +1 Time instead. Raising it past the top raises Bloodlust instead.

Time Core

Once Time drops below zero, the Action phase ends after the current action and nothing can bring it back up. Sitting on exactly zero is fine.

Movement and Victims Core

Move only along connecting lines. Leaving a space, up to 2 Victims may come with you, but never into the Killer’s space. Victims already in the Killer’s space will follow you out.

Saving Victims Core

Whenever you are on a green Exit space during the Action phase, even passing through, you may save Victims there. Put them on your Final Girl card and take the rewards they cover. Fill the card to flip it and unlock the Ultimate Ability; later saves give the reward under it. Victims alone on an Exit aren’t saved.

Searching Core

Play a Search card on an orange Search space. Draw from that space’s Item deck per the card, keep one, and return the rest on top faceup or bottom facedown. Never flip a facedown top card. An empty deck has nothing left to find.

Items and weapons Core

  • Two hands; a two-handed item fills both. The backpack holds anything, but hand items there can’t be used.
  • Rearrange only when you gain an item or in Upkeep.
  • Weapon range is exact: 0 means same space; 1+ means exactly that far from the Killer.
  • If an attack card deals 1+ damage, add one weapon’s modifier. Only one weapon per attack.
  • Limited-use items get Tracking markers; discard at zero.

Resting Core

Recover 1 health per heart, never above starting health. At Shady Acres, any heart effect can be spent on Madness instead.

Killer targeting and movement Core

  • Target the closest valid option. Ties go to the group with the most Victims.
  • Moves follow the shortest path up to his Movement Value per move icon, stopping on reaching the target.
  • Each attack icon is one attack at his Attack Value. Victims in his space are attacked before you unless you are the named target.
  • If he can’t reach his target but ends with someone else, he attacks them anyway.

Victims dying and Bloodlust Core

Victims have 1 health. Each death goes to the Dead space and raises Bloodlust by 1, applying that row’s effect. At max, each further rise triggers the final effect. Extra damage on a Victim is wasted.

Reaction cards Core

Blue Reaction cards are played only against an attack on you. One card per attack, but you may stack several on the same attack. They can’t protect Victims or stop unpreventable health loss.

Final Health tokens Core

When only the black token remains and more damage comes, flip it. Blank means dead. Hearts mean back to life: swap in a white token plus that many Health markers. Excess damage is ignored and the current phase ends immediately. Losing the white token is final.

Adrenaline Rush Core

+1 die on Horror Rolls while you or the Killer are down to only a Final Health token; +2 if both are.

Events and Special Victims Core

New Events don’t cancel old ones. An Event that can’t apply is ignored, with no replacement draw. Special Victims count as normal Victims unless their card says otherwise.

Enemies, Killer, Minions Core

“Enemy” rules cover everything hostile (Grimlash and the Children). “Killer” means Grimlash only. Children aren’t Minions, so Minion rules don’t apply to them unless their card says so.

Card terms Core

A card discarded from a deck top isn’t resolved. A card telling you to discard itself skips everything below that line. “Removed from the game” goes back in the box; “removed from play” can return.
